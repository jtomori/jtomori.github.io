**Efficient multi view data storage** [ <- ](..) _2025-09-02_ Working with volumetric video data (or any other multi-view data) is demanding on storage and can slow things down quite a bit (and make them expensive). In this post I'll explore ways of significantly improving that. # TL;DR The following steps can achieve a compression ratio of **18:1** on a typical volumetric video source footage. This can result in significant savings on the required hardware (or cloud storage costs) and increased processing throughput. The process is lossless in the areas we care about - the subject of our capture - and transparent to downstream applications.
